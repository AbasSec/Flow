# Flow Connect — P0 Communication Policy Template

> Use this as the visible organisation policy for the seeded demo tenant. Production organisations should receive a reviewable policy/editor flow and jurisdiction-specific advice before claiming legal compliance.

## Purpose

Flow Connect is an internal operational communication workspace. It helps our team coordinate work, share handovers, discuss orders/tasks/incidents, and make accountable operational decisions.

## Who can access it

Only active registered members of this organisation can access Flow Connect rooms assigned to them.

## Direct Work Conversations

Direct Work Conversations are company-managed operational conversations. They are not personal end-to-end encrypted messaging.

Authorised Organisation Owners and Organisation Administrators may review conversations when needed for operational continuity, security, safety, compliance, incident management, or investigation. Reviews require a reason and are recorded in Flow’s audit history.

## What should not be sent

Do not share:

- Card numbers, passwords, API keys, or security secrets.
- Sensitive customer information outside the authorised work thread.
- Harassment, discriminatory material, illegal content, or unrelated sensitive personal information.
- Informal instructions intended to bypass Flow’s approvals, payment, inventory, or safety controls.

## Formal actions

A message does not approve a discount, cancel an order, change stock, complete payment, change a role, or modify history. Use the relevant Flow action and approval process.

## Retention

Messages and audit data are retained according to the organisation’s configured retention policy. Deleting a message may remove it from ordinary display but does not automatically erase required audit or moderation evidence.

## Questions

Contact the Organisation Owner or Organisation Admin through the designated administrative channel.
